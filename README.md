## 📌 Project Overview
This project is designed to build a **Digital Literacy Portfolio** that enhances essential skills required in the modern digital era. It focuses on developing awareness about digital tools, creating a professional identity online, practicing effective communication, and understanding cyber safety.  

The project is divided into multiple structured tasks, each targeting a specific area such as digital awareness, portfolio creation, collaboration platforms, communication etiquette, and cybersecurity practices.  

---

## 🧾 Introduction
As part of the Digital Literacy course, this project aims to develop practical knowledge and awareness about responsible digital usage. In today’s fast-paced technological environment, students interact with multiple digital platforms daily, but often lack proper understanding of their efficient and secure usage.  

This portfolio highlights the importance of maintaining a professional digital presence, using tools productively, and ensuring safe online practices.  

The project consists of five tasks, each focusing on a key aspect of digital literacy. Through this work, I aim to improve my technical skills while also promoting awareness about safe and ethical digital behavior among others.

### 🔐 Task 5: Cybercrime Awareness
- Studied common cyber threats:
  - Phishing attacks  
  - Fake job/internship scams  
  - UPI/payment fraud  

**Key Takeaway:**  
Even small mistakes in digital platforms can lead to serious consequences.  

**Safety Practices:**  
- Always verify links before clicking  
- Never share OTP or PIN  
- Avoid scanning unknown QR codes  
- Use strong passwords and enable 2FA
