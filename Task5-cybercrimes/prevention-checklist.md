# Prevention Checklist for UPI / Online Payment Fraud

## ✅ Safety Tips

- Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, or bank details with anyone, even if they claim to be from your bank.
- Do not accept unknown “collect requests” on UPI apps without verifying the sender.
- Always remember: **receiving money does NOT require entering your UPI PIN**.
- Avoid scanning QR codes from unknown or untrusted sources.
- Double-check the receiver’s name before sending money.
- Do not click on suspicious links received via SMS, email, or WhatsApp.
- Use official and secure payment apps only (like Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).
- Enable app lock or biometric authentication on your payment apps.
- Keep your phone and apps updated to avoid security vulnerabilities.
- Avoid using public Wi-Fi while making financial transactions.

## 🔐 Specific Tips for Financial / UPI Safety

- Always verify UPI IDs before sending money (e.g., check name displayed in app).
- Set a strong UPI PIN and change it regularly.
- Immediately decline any unexpected payment request.
- Link your bank account only with trusted UPI apps.
- Enable SMS/email alerts for every transaction to monitor activity.

## 🚨 Reporting Cybercrime

If you become a victim of cyber fraud, report it immediately:

- 🌐 **National Cyber Crime Portal:** https://www.cybercrime.gov.in  
- 📞 **Helpline Number:** 1930  

> Quick reporting increases the chances of recovering lost money.

# Reflection (100–150 words)

While researching about UPI fraud, what surprised me the most was how easily people can be tricked into sending money without realizing it. Many victims believe they are receiving money, but in reality, they are approving a payment request. The speed at which these transactions happen and the difficulty in reversing them makes this type of fraud even more dangerous.

I also learned that cybercriminals often rely more on psychological manipulation than technical hacking. This made me realize the importance of awareness and presence of mind while using digital payment systems.

One habit I will personally change is being more cautious before approving any payment request or scanning QR codes. I will always verify the source and purpose before taking any action to ensure my financial safety.
